Frequently asked questions and technical limitations

            

              Which simulator platforms are supported?
              
TaxiNow supports Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 on Windows.

            

            

              Why is X-Plane not supported?
              
I do not fly with X-Plane regularly and I am not familiar enough with the X-Plane SDK to provide reliable support. I currently have no plans for X-Plane simulator integration, but I may consider it in the future if there is sufficient demand. This is separate from TaxiNow using selected X-Plane Scenery Gateway data as a fallback map-data source.

            

            

              How do I report an issue or submit feedback?
              

                Please leave a comment on TaxiNow's official
                flightsim.to
                page. Include the airport ICAO, simulator version, TaxiNow version and internal build number, and a screenshot when useful.
              

            

            

              Will you charge me for TaxiNow in the future?
              
No. TaxiNow is 100% free and I intend to keep it free. TaxiNow uses open-source data and technology, and charging for access could constitute commercial use. I created TaxiNow as a free tool and a gift to my fellow flight-simulation enthusiasts, and I will keep it free to the best of my ability.

            

            

              Do you collect any data? Is TaxiNow safe to use?
              
I do not collect or upload personal information, simulator telemetry, usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostic data. Aircraft position remains on your computer and local network. TaxiNow does not need a continuous internet connection; internet access is required only when downloading map assets. During a download, TaxiNow sends the requested airport or region to the relevant third-party map-data provider or official TaxiNow map-pack host.

              
I do not currently make the source code public in order to reduce unauthorized modification and redistribution. Qualified independent security or privacy experts may request limited source-review access solely to verify TaxiNow's security and privacy. This does not grant modification, disclosure, or redistribution rights.

            

            

              Thank you for making TaxiNow. Can I donate to you?
              
Thank you for your kindness. I do not currently accept donations, so TaxiNow can remain a purely free project. A five-star review and constructive feedback are always greatly appreciated.

            

            

              Can TaxiNow guarantee every airport map is correct?
              
No. TaxiNow combines OpenStreetMap, official aviation GIS where available, open global building footprints, OurAirports, and selected X-Plane operational details. Source coverage, age, and precision vary by airport and country. TaxiNow cannot guarantee that every runway, taxiway, stand, terminal outline, or label is complete or correct. TaxiNow does not maintain manual per-airport drawing corrections.

            

            

              Why can a terminal shape or “Terminal X” label be missing?
              
Some source datasets contain only an unnamed building footprint, only a terminal name point, or neither. TaxiNow attempts to match buildings to gates, aprons, terminal labels, and nearby accepted terminal geometry, but it will not invent a terminal number when no reliable name or number exists.

            

            

              Why do I see missing or broken terminal buildings?
              
First check the map source shown at the bottom of the map. If it shows X-Plane Scenery Gateway, TaxiNow used fallback data because it could not obtain usable OpenStreetMap data. Delete the airport from Downloaded Asset and download it again; a retry can sometimes obtain more complete OSM terminal geometry.

              

                If several downloads still use X-Plane Scenery Gateway, usable OSM airport data is probably unavailable. X-Plane airport data may contain missing, unnamed, or fragmented terminal shapes. See also
                Can TaxiNow guarantee every airport map is correct?
              

            

            

              Why is my aircraft position out of sync after using warp?
              
This can be normal because the simulator may not publish the updated aircraft position immediately after a warp or teleport. First click the position-refresh button above the zoom controls. If the position is still stale, begin moving the aircraft; the map should update when the simulator sends its next position change.

            

            

              Can I treat a planned taxi route as an ATC clearance?
              
No. Automatic routes use a generic pathfinding algorithm and might not reflect the real taxi route. The planner cannot reliably account for taxiway direction, wingspan and aircraft-size limits, temporary closures, local procedures, or live ATC instructions. Always follow ATC and airport signage. If you do not like an automatic route, use Enter Customized Taxi Route to highlight the route you intend to follow.

            

            

              Is TaxiNow suitable for real-world navigation?
              
No. Maps may be incomplete, outdated, inferred, or inaccurate. TaxiNow is exclusively for personal flight-simulator use.

            

            

              There is another app called "NavApron" that looks so similar to TaxiNow; is NavApron related to TaxiNow?/Did you develop NavApron?
              
No. I did not develop NavApron, and I have not authorized anyone to create a work derived from TaxiNow. To my knowledge, NavApron appeared within a week of TaxiNow V1.0 Beta and within four days of TaxiNow V1.1. It is understood that timing and appearance alone cannot prove how another product was developed.

              
However, the unusually close timing, similar UI/UX, apparently AI-generated features, OSM data query and processing logic, and prototype artifacts resembling issues I manually corrected in TaxiNow, including overlapping taxiway markers and level-of-detail transitions, give me reason to suspect that TaxiNow may have been used as a reference for a distilled or derivative work. That is my assessment, not a verified finding. NavApron also places prominent, visually disruptive donation buttons in its main interface. I otherwise have no comment on that project.

              
TaxiNow remains copyrighted software despite being free. It may not be copied, derived from, reverse engineered, or supplied to AI tools for distillation. If you want to build a better tool using methods or technology demonstrated in TaxiNow, contact me through GitHub. I am willing to discuss parts of my methodology when proper credit is given. Commercial use, including soliciting donations, is strictly prohibited for any work using TaxiNow code, assets, or protected technology. I have invested substantial time and resources in creating TaxiNow as freeware, and I ask other developers to respect that work and contact me directly before attempting to reuse it.
